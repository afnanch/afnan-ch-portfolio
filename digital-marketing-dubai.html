How we plan and measure the work

  1. Understand the business. Share your website, audience, products or services, existing channels and immediate problem. We decide whether the work is a good fit.
  2. Agree a baseline and scope. Record the current pages, content or campaigns and the metrics available. Agree deliverables, review points, responsibilities, fees and separate costs.
  3. Make the agreed changes. Work on the highest-impact issues first. Review content for factual accuracy and approve campaign budgets before spend.
  4. Review evidence. Compare the same measures over time, explain what changed and choose the next step from the evidence rather than a ranking promise.

For SEO, useful measures include indexing, relevant search impressions, clicks and enquiries. For social content, they include reach, engagement and relevant conversations. For paid campaigns, they include agreed conversion events and cost per useful result. An increase in one metric does not automatically prove an increase in revenue.
