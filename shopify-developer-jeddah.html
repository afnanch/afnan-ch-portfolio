Jeddah specifics to plan before a build

  • Arabic-first or bilingual: settle this before choosing a theme. Right-to-left affects menus, sliders, cart drawers and checkout, and the wrong theme makes retrofitting expensive.
  • Payments: mada is the default card expectation, with Apple Pay and STC Pay close behind. Shopify Payments is not available in Saudi Arabia at the time of writing, so stores use gateways such as Tap, PayTabs or HyperPay - approval needs your commercial registration, so start it early.
  • Instalments: Tabby and Tamara are expected in fashion, beauty, electronics and gifting. Their approval is separate from your gateway's.
  • Delivery: zones and promised timeframes should match what SMSA, Aramex or Saudi Post actually deliver across Jeddah's districts and onward to Makkah, Madinah and the rest of the Kingdom, with cash-on-delivery limits set deliberately.
  • Tax: 15% VAT with your ZATCA-registered number, shown correctly on prices and invoices.
