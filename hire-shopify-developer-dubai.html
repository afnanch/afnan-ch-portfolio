How to Hire a Shopify Developer in Dubai in 2026

The short answer: decide what you actually need built first, expect AED 3,000-10,000 for a solid freelancer store setup and AED 15,000+ for custom work, and filter candidates with a handful of questions that quickly separate real developers from theme installers. Here is the honest guide.

What a Shopify developer actually does

The title covers three different jobs, and mixing them up is how budgets go wrong. A store setup is configuring a theme, loading your catalog, connecting payments and delivery, writing policy pages and basic SEO - days of work, not weeks. A custom build is design and theme development: a unique look, custom sections, migrations from another platform, Arabic/English setups. App or integration work is real software development: private apps, API connections to your inventory or accounting, custom checkout logic. A store that needs only the first job should not pay for the third.

What it costs in Dubai (honest ranges)

  • Standard store setup by a freelancer: roughly AED 3,000-10,000 depending on catalog size, content quality and how much is written for you versus pasted in.
  • Custom design or complex builds: commonly AED 10,000-25,000+, with agencies at the higher end and timelines of weeks.
  • Ongoing help: small retainers of AED 500-2,000 per month cover updates, small changes and someone to call; many stores need far less than they are sold.

Platform costs (plan, apps, domain) are separate and continue after launch - see my full Shopify cost breakdown for the UAE before budgeting the build.

Local agency, local freelancer, or remote?

A Dubai agency gives you a contracts department and a project manager, and charges for both. A local freelancer costs less and you meet face to face. A remote specialist costs less still, and for Shopify work almost everything - theme code, apps, payments setup, training over a call - happens inside the browser anyway. The honest trade-off with remote is communication discipline: insist on written scope, staged payments and weekly progress you can see on a live preview link. Whichever route you choose, those three habits matter more than geography.

Questions that filter out bad hires

  • "Show me three live stores you built and what exactly you did on each." Then visit them on your phone.
  • "Will I own the store, domain and ad accounts in my name?" The only acceptable answer is yes, with them added as staff.
  • "What will my monthly running cost be after launch?" A developer who cannot estimate this has not built many stores.
  • "How do you handle payments approval?" Anyone who guarantees gateway approval before seeing your trade licence documents is guessing.
  • "What happens after launch - what is included and what is billed?" Get it in writing.

Red flags worth walking away from

Guaranteed sales or guaranteed search rankings. Quotes that bundle everything into one number with no monthly breakdown. Portfolios of screenshots instead of live URLs. Hosting your store inside their own Shopify account so you cannot leave. A "custom theme" quote for what is actually a paid theme with your logo. And pressure to pay the full amount upfront - staged payments tied to visible progress are standard practice.

A sensible sequence

  1. Write down what you sell, roughly how many products, and who updates the store after launch.
  2. Collect two or three quotes with the same written scope, so you are comparing like with like.
  3. Start with a free theme and a focused catalog; upgrade only when a real limitation appears.
  4. Pay in stages: start, preview approval, launch.
  5. Keep ownership of everything - domain, store, ad accounts, analytics.

Common questions

How much does a Shopify developer charge in Dubai?

For a standard store setup - theme configured, catalog loaded, payments and delivery connected, policies written, basic SEO - freelancer quotes usually fall between AED 3,000 and AED 10,000. Custom design, bilingual builds or integrations commonly run AED 10,000-25,000 or more, with agencies at the higher end. Be suspicious of any quote that cannot say what the monthly running cost will be after launch.

Should I hire a local Dubai developer or work remotely?

Shopify work happens almost entirely in the browser, so remote specialists deliver the same store for less; Dubai agencies charge more and add account management. Whichever you pick, the habits that protect you are identical: written scope, staged payments tied to visible progress, live preview links, and all accounts owned by your business.

How long does a Shopify store take to build?

A standard store setup is usually one to three weeks including content and revisions, assuming your product photos and details are ready. Custom design or bilingual builds run four to eight weeks. The most common delay is not development at all - it is waiting on product content and payment gateway approval, so start both early.

Do I need a trade licence before hiring a developer?

You can plan and even build the store first, but payment gateway approval in the UAE requires your trade licence and business documents, so the store cannot take real payments without them. If your licence is in process, tell the developer on day one - it changes the launch sequence, not the build.
