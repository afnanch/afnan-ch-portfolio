How Much Does Digital Marketing Cost in Dubai and the UAE in 2026?

The short answer: a small UAE business typically spends AED 3,000-10,000 per month on digital marketing done properly, and anything promising first place on Google for a few hundred dirhams is not marketing. Here is the honest breakdown, line by line.

SEO (monthly)

In Dubai, freelancer SEO typically runs AED 1,500-5,000 per month and agencies AED 3,000-10,000+, depending on competition and how much content is included. What moves the price: your industry's competition (real estate and clinics are expensive, niche services are not), the state of your website, and whether you need English only or English plus Arabic. Expect three to six months before judging results, and treat any guaranteed ranking as a warning sign - no agency controls Google's results.

Google Ads (management plus budget)

Management fees run AED 1,000-3,000 per month for small accounts, or 10-20% of spend for larger ones. The budget itself goes to Google: Dubai cost-per-click ranges from about AED 2-8 for less competitive local services to AED 15-50+ for real estate, legal and medical terms. A realistic starting test budget for a small service business is AED 2,000-5,000 per month. Ads produce enquiries immediately but stop when you stop paying - pair them with SEO if you want compounding returns.

Social media management (monthly)

AED 1,500-5,000 per month covers most small-business packages in the UAE, with the range driven by posting volume, video production and whether ad management is included. Short video (Reels, TikTok) is where attention actually is in the Gulf, but shoot days and editing are often billed separately - ask what a package includes in writing before signing.

Website (one-time)

A clean business website in the UAE costs roughly AED 3,000-10,000 from a freelancer and AED 10,000-25,000+ from agencies; e-commerce builds start higher. Selling online? Read the full Shopify store cost breakdown for the UAE before budgeting. Whatever you pay, make sure the domain, hosting and analytics accounts are registered to your business, not the provider.

Remote vs local agency: an honest note

A Dubai agency charges Dubai rates partly because of Dubai overheads. A senior freelancer working remotely from a lower-cost market can do the same SEO, ads and development work for noticeably less, and video calls cover most collaboration. The trade-offs are timezone overlap (the Gulf and India are close, so this is minor), in-person meetings, and local-language nuance - for Arabic-first campaigns, budget for a native Arabic copywriter either way.

How to spot a bad quote in Dubai

Be careful with guaranteed rankings, packages that never name deliverables, reports full of impressions but no enquiry numbers, and any setup where your Google Business Profile, ad account or website lives in the agency's name. Your business should own every account, with access granted to whoever you hire.
