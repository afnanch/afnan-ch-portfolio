A practical Shopify build scope

Discovery and store structure

We review your product types, catalog size, variants, collection logic and customer questions. A simple store and a large migration are different jobs. Before a quote, we identify the source of your product information, what needs cleaning and who will approve it.

Theme and product pages

The work can include theme configuration, brand styling, home and collection layouts, product-page content and mobile navigation. A theme or app is selected for a real requirement, not installed to make a feature list longer. Custom functionality is scoped before it is promised.

Migration and integrations

If you have an existing store, we first review available exports, URL changes, product images, variants and the data that needs to be carried across. Customer and order information require careful access handling. Any migration, redirect plan or external integration gets an agreed scope and test plan before changes to the live store.

SEO foundations and handover

Titles, descriptions, collection structure, useful product content and internal links are part of a sound store foundation. We can review sitemap access and search-console setup where authorised. These steps help search engines understand a store; they do not guarantee first-place rankings or sales.

Handover should make the agreed setup understandable: where product information lives, which apps are active, what recurring costs exist and which parts need ongoing maintenance.

What to review before launch

  • Mobile shopping: readable product information, usable menus, variant selection and clear calls to action.
  • Catalog quality: accurate prices, product descriptions, images, stock behaviour and collection membership.
  • Business settings: owner-approved currency, delivery areas, returns/contact information and policy pages.
  • Checkout: the agreed test route, payment-provider setup and delivery configuration. Live payment approval is the provider's decision.
  • Search continuity: important existing URLs and any agreed redirects when replacing a site.
  • Ownership: the business retains control of its account, domains and billing, with access granted only as needed.

Launch scope, testing and ongoing support are agreed for each project. I will flag work that depends on a provider, translator or specialist rather than pretend it is already included.
