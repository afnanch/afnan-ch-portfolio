How a project runs

  1. Discovery: you share your products, any current website, stores you like and the customers you want.
  2. Scope and quote: we agree pages, catalogue work, integrations and price in writing, plus the recurring platform costs, before anything starts.
  3. Build and review: you check the design, mobile layout, product pages and settings as they come together.
  4. Launch and handover: we test the customer journey end to end, and the store is handed over with access fully under your control.

Common questions

Do I need to be in Kozhikode to work with you?

No. Most projects run remotely over calls and screen shares, so I work with businesses anywhere in Kerala. In-person meetings are possible around Kozhikode and Malappuram when a project calls for it.

Can you take over a store someone else built?

Usually, yes. I start with a review of the existing store - theme, apps, catalogue and settings - then we agree which fixes are worth doing before any changes are made.

What should I prepare before contacting a Shopify developer?

A product list or catalogue, any branding you have, a few stores you like, and a rough idea of your budget and launch date. With that, I can come back with a realistic scope instead of a guess.

How much does a Shopify store cost in Kerala?

The build quote depends on design, catalogue size, integrations and any migration work. Shopify's subscription, paid themes and app charges are separate ongoing costs. For full 2026 numbers, read my Shopify store cost breakdown for Kerala.

Will my store rank on Google and get sales immediately?

No honest developer can promise that. A clean store structure and useful content give you a fair chance, but rankings and sales depend on competition, demand, products and marketing. I will never quote you a guaranteed position.
