How Much Does Digital Marketing Cost in Saudi Arabia in 2026?

The short answer: a small Saudi business typically spends SAR 3,000-10,000 per month on digital marketing done properly, and anything promising first place on Google for a few hundred riyals is not marketing. Here is the honest breakdown, line by line.

SEO (monthly)

In Saudi Arabia, freelancer SEO typically runs SAR 1,500-5,000 per month and agencies SAR 3,000-10,000+, depending on your market and how much content is included. What moves the price: competition in your city and industry (Riyadh real estate and Jeddah clinics are expensive; niche services are not), the state of your website, and whether you need Arabic content, English content or both. Expect three to six months before judging results, and treat any guaranteed ranking as a warning sign - no agency controls Google's results.

Google Ads (management plus budget)

Management fees run SAR 1,000-3,000 per month for small accounts, or 10-20% of spend for larger ones. The budget itself goes to Google: Saudi cost-per-click ranges from a couple of riyals for less competitive local services to SAR 15-50+ for real estate, legal and medical terms. A realistic starting test budget for a small service business is SAR 2,000-5,000 per month. Plan around the calendar: competition and click prices rise sharply in Ramadan and around Eid, when buying intent is highest, so the same budget buys less then - but converts better. Ads produce enquiries immediately and stop when you stop paying; pair them with SEO for compounding returns.

Social media management (monthly)

SAR 1,500-5,000 per month covers most small-business packages, with the range driven by posting volume, video production and whether ad management is included. One Saudi-specific note: Snapchat and TikTok carry unusual weight in the Kingdom compared with many markets, alongside Instagram - a package that ignores them is missing where Saudi attention actually is. Shoot days and editing are often billed separately, so ask what a package includes in writing before signing.

Website (one-time)

A clean business website costs roughly SAR 3,000-10,000 from a freelancer and SAR 12,000-30,000+ from agencies; e-commerce builds start higher, especially Arabic-first stores with proper right-to-left layouts. Selling online? Read the full Shopify store cost breakdown for Saudi Arabia and my guide to hiring a Shopify developer in Saudi Arabia before budgeting. Whatever you pay, make sure the domain, hosting and analytics accounts are registered to your business, not the provider.

Remote vs local agency: an honest note

A Riyadh or Jeddah agency charges big-city rates partly because of big-city overheads. A senior freelancer working remotely from a lower-cost market can do the same SEO, ads and development work for noticeably less, and the Gulf-India timezone gap is small enough that calls are easy. The real trade-offs are in-person meetings and local-language nuance - for Arabic-first campaigns, budget for a native Arabic copywriter whichever route you choose.

How to spot a bad quote in Saudi Arabia

Be careful with guaranteed rankings, packages that never name deliverables, reports full of impressions but no enquiry numbers, and any setup where your Google Business Profile, ad account or website lives in the agency's name. Your business should own every account, with access granted to whoever you hire.
