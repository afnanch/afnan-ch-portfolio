How Much Does a Shopify Store Cost in Kerala in 2026?

The short answer: a small Kerala store can run on roughly Rs 4,000-12,000 per month in platform and app costs, plus a one-time build that ranges from a free DIY weekend to Rs 60,000 or more for a custom project. Here is the honest breakdown, line by line.

Shopify plan fees (recurring)

Shopify bills in US dollars even for Indian stores. At the time of writing, the published pricing is Basic at $39 per month, the mid plan at $105 and Advanced at $399, with roughly 25% off each when paid yearly. Prices change, so check shopify.com/pricing before you budget - and watch your card statement, because the rupee amount moves with the exchange rate.

At roughly Rs 84-85 to the dollar, Basic works out near Rs 3,300 per month. Most Kerala stores starting out fit Basic comfortably. The mid plan only makes sense when staff accounts and reporting matter, and Advanced mainly when lower transaction rates pay for themselves at higher volumes.

Domain and theme (small, mostly one-time)

  • Domain: about Rs 800-1,300 per year for a .com, less for a .in. Buy it in your own account so the business owns it, never in the developer's.
  • Theme: the free Dawn theme is genuinely capable and where I suggest most stores start. Paid themes run about $180-350 one-time, and only earn their cost when they solve a specific layout need you have already identified.

Apps (recurring, easy to underestimate)

Reviews, WhatsApp chat, a courier aggregator and email capture cover most early needs. Several solid options have free tiers, and a typical small Kerala store spends Rs 0-4,000 per month here. The trap is installing an app for every idea; each paid app is a monthly commitment, so review the list every quarter and remove what stopped earning its place.

Payments in India

Kerala buyers expect UPI first, then cards, and cash on delivery still matters outside the cities. Razorpay, PayU and Cashfree are the gateways most Indian Shopify stores use, each charging around 2% per domestic transaction plus GST on their fees. Approval needs your business documents - PAN, bank account and usually a GST number - so budget days for approval, not just money. COD orders add courier-side collection fees, typically Rs 30-60 per order, which is worth pricing into your margins.

GST and registration (not optional)

If you sell online across state lines, GST registration is generally required regardless of turnover, and your invoices must carry your GSTIN. The GST rate itself depends on what you sell - clothing, food and electronics each sit in different slabs. Registration is free on the government portal, though many owners pay an accountant Rs 2,000-5,000 to set it up and handle filings. Confirm your exact obligations with a tax professional, because the rules depend on your products and turnover.

Development cost (the widest range)

A DIY build on Dawn costs only your time, and for a focused catalog that is a legitimate start. A freelancer setup - theme configured, products loaded, payments and shipping tested, basic SEO in place - typically runs Rs 15,000-60,000 depending on catalog size and how much content you supply. Custom design, Malayalam-English bilingual content or migration from another platform adds scope. Anyone quoting a single flat price before seeing your products is guessing.

A realistic first-year budget for a small Kerala store

Adding it up: plan fees near Rs 40,000 per year, a domain near Rs 1,000, apps from zero to Rs 25,000, gateway fees near 2% of sales, and a one-time build between free and Rs 60,000. A careful first year lands between roughly Rs 50,000 and Rs 1.5 lakh all-in, excluding your own time and ad spend. Stores that skip the planning stage usually spend the same money less visibly - on apps they did not need and rework they could have avoided.

Common questions

What is the minimum monthly cost to run a Shopify store in Kerala?

Around Rs 3,500-8,000 per month covers the Basic plan plus a few essential apps for a small store, with the domain adding under Rs 1,500 per year. The one-time build ranges from a free DIY weekend to Rs 60,000 or more for a custom project.

Does Shopify bill in rupees?

No - Shopify bills in US dollars, so the rupee amount on your card varies slightly with the exchange rate. Check shopify.com/pricing for current plan prices before budgeting, since prices change.

Do I need GST registration to sell on Shopify from Kerala?

Online sellers shipping across state lines generally need GST registration regardless of turnover, and payment gateways ask for a GST number during approval. The rate you charge depends on your product category. Confirm your specific case with a tax professional.

Should a Kerala business pick Shopify or an Indian platform?

It depends on where you sell. Indian platforms bundle local logistics and feel cheaper at small volumes; Shopify earns its cost when you want full design control, international sales or a store that can grow past the local market. I build on both kinds and will say honestly when Shopify is the wrong fit.
